# The teleprompter

**View > Teleprompter** opens the talk in a window of its own, in big text that moves by itself. It opens on a second screen when there is one. It follows your plan: your speed, the pauses and the marks of the talk.

## Start and stop

- `Space` starts and stops. A short countdown comes first (see Options below); `Esc` or `Space` cancels it.
- `←` and `→` (or Page Up and Page Down, so that a presentation clicker works) go to the previous or the next paragraph. `R` or `Home` starts again.
- **Automatic** moves the text by itself; **Step by step** moves it only when you press a key.

## Speed

The speed is a share of your planned pace: 100 % is the plan, 50 % takes twice as long, 300 % is three times as fast. The bar at the bottom shows it as a percentage and as words per minute. Change it with the bar, with `↑` and `↓` (or `+` and `-`), or with `Ctrl` and the mouse wheel. It can be changed while the text moves.

## How the text moves

**Options** > **Text movement** has two ways:

- **Follows the plan**: each line stays on the screen for the time the plan gives its words. So the speed is not the same everywhere: it slows down around a pause and picks up again over a few seconds, slow and fast marks change it, and short lines and headings that are not read aloud pass quickly. The pause countdown and the section in the clock always agree with the text. At a high speed these differences are easier to see.
- **Constant speed**: the text moves at an even speed. Pauses still stop it, for as many seconds as planned, and at 100 % the talk still ends when the plan says. Slow and fast marks and the length of the lines no longer change the speed of the text. Between two pauses, the section and the pause countdown in the clock can differ by a few seconds from the line on the screen.

In both ways the text starts and slows down smoothly, without jumps.

## Move around

- The mouse wheel moves the reading line; a click on a line puts the reading line there.
- **Sections** (or `J`) lists the headings; `↑`, `↓` and `Enter`, or a click, jump to one.

## Options

`O` or **Options** has: **Focus band** (the text fades away from the reading line), **Countdown before start**, **Finish by** (the time of day the talk must be over by: the clock then shows when it will end, and how early or late that is), **Text movement**, **Text width**, **Line spacing** and **Colors** (yellow, green or white on black, or black on white; with these colors **Light** and **Dark** on the bar do nothing). **Reset display** puts the width, the spacing and the colors back.

On the bar there are also **Mirror** (left to right) and **Flip** (upside down), for the glass of a prompter (with **Flip** the line being read is near the bottom, because the text comes from above), **Light** and **Dark**, **Clock**, **Full screen** and **Rehearse** (see **Speech marks**).
