# Spanish punctuation help

In Spanish a question or exclamation opens with a mark as well as closing with one: *¿Cómo estás?* *¡Qué bien!*

## Hints for the opening marks

When a sentence ends with `?` or `!` and no `¿` or `¡` opened it, Caret underlines the last word and the mark with a dotted blue line. Right-click it:

- **Add ¿ at the start of the sentence**, and
- **Add ¿ after the comma**, when the sentence has one (*Pedro, ¿dime cómo estás?*).

Caret never picks the place for you, because a sentence may start after a name or an *if* clause, and it never types anything unless you choose it. Nothing is added while you write.

The hints are on by default when the interface is in Spanish. **Settings > Editor > Opening ¿ and ¡ hints** turns them on or off. They read the Visual view only. English words with a `?` inside a Spanish text can be underlined too: turn the setting off if that gets in the way. Code, web addresses and links are left alone.

## Marks that are not on every keyboard

**Edit > Insert punctuation** types, at the caret, in the editor and in the Code view:

- ¿ opening question mark
- ¡ opening exclamation mark
- « and » angle quotation marks
- — the dialogue dash
